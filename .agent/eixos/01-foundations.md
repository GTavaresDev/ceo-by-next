# Axis 01: Foundations

Source: NEST course, Chapter 1 ("Why is SEO so important?")

## Why SEO matters

SEO is the key to increased conversion and confidence in your brand. A higher search ranking placement means more organic visitors.

Organic search traffic (visitors who arrive by clicking a result in a search engine) matters for three reasons:

- **Qualitative:** visitors are more likely to become customers.
- **Trustable:** organic results build more confidence in the brand or mission than paid placements.
- **Low-cost:** good SEO practices that improve ranking are free. There is no direct cost to appear in top organic results, apart from the time and effort invested.

## SEO vs SEM

- **SEO (Search Engine Optimization):** improves organic (unpaid) results.
- **SEM (Search Engine Marketing):** paid placements. Top paid results are labeled `Sponsored`.

## The three pillars of optimization

| Pillar | Goal | Typical work |
|--------|------|--------------|
| **Technical** | Make the site easy to crawl and fast | Crawlability, indexing rules, web performance, rendering |
| **Creation** | Target the right keywords | Content strategy, keyword targeting |
| **Popularity** | Show search engines the site is trusted | Backlinks from third-party sites |

## Scope for a Next.js developer

SEO is a broad discipline with many facets. As a Next.js developer, the first step is to make the web app SEO-ready by applying the technical best practices covered in axes 02 to 04.

## Key takeaways

- Organic traffic is free, trustworthy and high-converting compared to paid results.
- SEO has three pillars. Technical work is the one developers control most directly.
- Start with the technical pillar: make the app crawlable, indexable and fast.
